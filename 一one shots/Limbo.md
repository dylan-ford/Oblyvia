A tear in reality has opened up due to a destabilized anchor stone within the chaotic plane and the players must venture inside and locate the source of the anomaly
- visitors to Limbo find themselves bombarded with intrusive thoughts and unruly emotions forcing them to confront the transient nature of so much of what they think of as their identity
- the key to success on the plane is in mastering the internal landscape of chaos and asserting one's sense of self and finding what is unchanging

Gameplay differences in Limbo
- Limbo has no gravity so creatures can move up to their movement speed in any direction
- As a magic action, a creature can make an Intelligence check to mentally move an object that is within 30 feet of itself that isn't being worn or carried
	- The DC depends on the size of the objects (5 tiny, 10 small, 15 medium, 20 large, 25 huge+)
	- On a success, the creature moves the object 5 feet + a number equal to the amount the exceeded the DC by
- As a magic action, a creature can make an intelligence check to alter a nonmagical object within 30 feet of itself that isn't being worn or carried.
	- DC depends on the size of the object: 10 tiny, 15 small, 20 medium, 25 for large+
	- On a success, the creature can turn the object into another nonliving form of the same size (boulder into a fireball)
- As a magic action, a creature can make a DC 20 intelligence check to stabilize an area within a 30-foot-radius sphere centered on a point it can see
	- On a success, the creature prevents the area from being altered by the plane for 24 hours or until the creature takes this magic action again
