An elegant spider goddess who's story tells of a seamstress who is forever tasked to weave the moon each month. When she completes it the darkness (creatures from the abyss?) slowly eats away at it until she must begin anew. Appearance Body contains patterns like constellations, and is always surrounded by lunar moths who carry her silk. Moth face? Some say the shooting stars seen in the night sky are her moths carrying her silk to faraway lands. 
Her high priestesses mummify themselves in silk to enter a trance where they believe they may feel her presence more strongly.
