Primordial Archfey of Dreams | 9

Appearance:


The altered state of consciousness while asleep produces a different quality of emotions then while awake. These emotions are uncontrolled, and raw, and require finesse to siphon. The mastery of this skill left Revalom as the last primordial archfey to appear in this world, but its domain is tightly secured.
Revalom has a more intimate influence over its vessels than the other archfey, being able to manipulate dreams directly. Blessing mortals with beautiful dreams that fulfill every waking desire or twisting them into wretched nightmares. The nightmares induce sleep deprivation and the dreams cause mortals to discard the waking world entirely. Either way the barrier between the waking world and the dream world blurs, and the thin planar veil keeping Revalom at bay shatters for a moment, long enough to plucks its victim from the mortal realm.
- Revalom can't physically exist in the mortal plane so it breaks down the barriers for an individual between our realm and the dream realm until the victim can be pulled in fully and consumed by Revalom
