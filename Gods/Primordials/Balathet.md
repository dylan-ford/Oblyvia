Primordial of Greed | 7

Balathet, the seventh of the primordial archfey, may also be the boldest. Balathet seeks out the ambitious and the bitter, revealing itself in the form of a salesman, a bargainer, a friend, offering to them the means to seize what they deserve, what they are owed! Vast power to conquer, immeasurable wealth, or perhaps a second chance? …All that's required is a small price, of course. The first taste of power burns bright, invigorating its victim with a scintillating constellation of possibilities… Then comes the itch. An eternally voracious ambition stirs within its victims—enough becomes sparse, plenty turns into scraps, and satisfaction becomes a fading memory. The curse itself grows equally greedy, invading more and more parts of the victim’s brain until all reason and inhibition have seceded. First, they forget their mother’s face. Then their own. Boney hands claw at their own skin, convinced their veins run with gold. They become a passenger in their own life, watching helplessly as they slave eternally away for more, always more. Balathet lingers ever nearby, whispering tales of wealthy kingdoms and untold riches just out of reach—feasting as each thump of its victim’s heartbeat becomes a down payment in their next pursuit. When the husk finally collapses, Balathet licks the final glint of avarice from its eye and begins seeking another.
