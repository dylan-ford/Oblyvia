Primordial of Agony | 5

A serrated leviathan of knitted bones and spines. A being that could eviscerate a town in its embrace, leaving gored streets of flayed sinew and shredded limbs in its wake.
Cekaxis, the primordial force of agony, revels in the torment released through excruciating pain. A creature of pure malice, it delights in making bargains by offering up powerful boons in exchange for wanton suffering. Many powerful cults worship Cekaxis, summoning its presence with the tortured screams of their victims
