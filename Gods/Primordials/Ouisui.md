Primordial of Revelry | 6

People seek out Ouesui as an escape from their undesirable material conditions; a way to get lost in the revelry of festivals, to reminisce in the glee of nostalgia, and to get drunk with the euphoria of excess. Ouesui bestows on these seekers a boon improving their charisma, allowing them to gather people for a party. Drink and revelry extend into the night and joyous extravagance is experienced… but as morning comes and the euphoria dissipates with the morning fog, the reminder of the hollowness of life fills the seekers again. So more parties must be thrown! Every night a celebration better than the last! The pure excess of these bouts snagging the downtrodden like fish to a lure, and the other townsfolk see the dysfunction for what it is. Hedonistic husks trading their souls for the ultimate avoidance. Minds consumed with getting drunk and high until ones problems drain away with their lives.
