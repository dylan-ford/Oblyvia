Primordial of Despair | 8

Sepulchur, The Grey Dirge is the eigth primordial archfey, a rotting drain in the basin of the world devouring it of its hope. Sepulchur seeps its rotting tendrils into the minds of the broken, planting seeds of doubt in their torn minds. These seeds fester, growing barbed roots like chains which coil around thoughts and memories. Victims begin to second guess themselves, and feel guilt and shame about what were once cheerful memories and thoughts. These tendrils do not choke at once, they tighten with every unanswered prayer, each door unopened, and with every word swallowed back into silence, until hope is not stolen but forgotten. Grief turns into a prison and loneliness becomes a tomb, where the darkest thoughts echo the loudest. When every flicker of hope has left, it offers the cruelest mercy: an eternal silence to the maddening echoes of despair.
Sepulchur is patient and aims to slowly but deeply drain its victims of hope. Unlike other archfey, who make a spectacle of their feasting rituals, Sepulchur prefers to siphon its victims over long periods. It does so through its faint whispers which reach the uncertain in their moments of regret. It acts as the ruminating voice in the back of their heads, slowly but surely convincing them they are broken like a spectral gardener tending to its garden. When their despair blooms, Sepulchur reveals itself as a friend, offering a merciful end and to guide them to the afterlife. This of course is one last trick to squeeze the last bit of despair from its victim as they realize the light at the end of the tunnel was the glint of the jaws of the beast.

- Appearance 
	- Extremely long head like Hollow knight crying statue or Scream mask with bottom of face extended creating a massive gaping frown
	- Long gills along side of head/neck
