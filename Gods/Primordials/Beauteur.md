Primordial of Ecstacy | 3

Beauteur, the alluring mirage, is the third of the primordial archfey and feeds on the raw and primal energy of ecstasy. A creature of impossible allure existing in the fine line between agony and rapture. Beauteur is an ever-shifting visage of seduction, a figure of sculpted perfection, skin glistening like oil on water. It slithers into the mind of its prey with whispers of soft silk, materializing their deepest lust until their pulse pounds loud enough to drown out reason. Beauteur appears before its prey, adorning itself with these fantasies, beckoning its prey like a siren. It shares in its prey's ecstasy, teasing and filling them with yearning as it salivates in anticipation. In the throes of climax, Beauteur primes itself to feast, slowly revealing its true form before devouring its prey's head.

Appearance
- Whatever its victim lusts for most; silky, oiled, smooth  
Modus
- Beauteur employs many tricks when its appetite has grown strong. It may orchestrate grandiose parties by influencing high ranking nobles of a city, devouring the most devout and puritanical who surrender to their carnal desires, for their ecstasy burns like a wildfire. Or it may slither its influence like tendrils into a cult, feeding on the storm of pleasure from their orgiastic worship like wine. Some accounts have told of desolate cities full of husks of bodies warped into positions of rapture and faces twisted in eternal smiles
