Lore
- 
- Her holy trees are constructed via tree shaping which leaves a sizable hollow in the middle to be filled by a pure vessel (usually a young virgin). There the vessel will remain still and be enveloped by the tree roots as they becomes one with the root system of the forest. Once transformation is complete, attuned druid elders can commune with these trees which  act as voices of the forest
