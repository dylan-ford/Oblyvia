Domain
- Balance
- Cleric: Peace
Lore
- Within her is a perpetual engine of balance, taking in prayers of those who have strayed too far in either direction of order and chaos and purifying the prayers into a liquid of pure balance.
- Her holy sites are wells of grace, of which drinking from is said to remove all curses, blights, and ailments and restore one to balance; guides rigid minds to relinquish control, and chaotic minds to find peace and let go of the past which binds them
Appearance
- Automaton-like body resembling a humanoid woman with skin of curved brass plates and an eternally neutral face. From her chest protrude jugs that are constantly flowing water out of her body.
