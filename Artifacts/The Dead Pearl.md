An ancient relic that once had the power to grant a single wish upon drinking deeply enough of the emotions of the world around it. The nature of the wish was shaped by the emotions it had absorbed. Most famously the pearl was in possession of the now long forgotten kingdom of [name], where it sat prominently enshrined in the town square. During festivals, the Pearl would capture the essence of revelry, high spirits, and joy and would bless the city with good fortune and harvest every year. Legend tells that the emperor's vizier conspired with a cabal that had long since wormed its way into the royal court. The king was dragged before his subjects and publicly executed, his death serving as a spectacle to scare the kingdom into submission, and an era of tyranny descended onto the realm. 
The citizens grew increasingly discontent under the brutal regime, and The Pearl drank deep of their sorrow. By the second year, the skies had opened, unleashing relentless rainfall onto the kingdom, painting its skies a grey tapestry from one horizon to the next. The vizier locked the Pearl in the castle's vaults, hoping to sever it from the misery of his subjects and end the rain. However, what really consumed him was the power the Pearl promised, and he sought to study it to bend it to his will. But meager stone walls couldn't prevent the unrest writhing within. 
While the vizier obsessed with the Pearl, his kingdom seethed. Civil unrest swelled into rebellion, and as their anger grew, the Pearl's hue darkened to a deep bloody ruby. Its glow brightened with every riot, its light pulsing slowly but furiously. Yet the vizier, enthralled by ambition, ordered his mages continue their work as the kingdom crumbled around him.
The sparks that now leapt from the Pearl were not an invitation to the new world the cabal would usher in, but the final warning from the one they destroyed. As the rebellion finally breached the castle walls, their emotions reached a fever pitch. The smothering grey veil over the heavens faded, and was replaced with a brilliant blazing crimson. In an instant, the Pearl unleashed the pent-up fury in a wave cataclysmic wrath, and the kingdom was unmade.
