Baba Yaga
Yama-uba
- cannibalistic crones
- mouth at the top of her head under her hair
- only weakness is a certain flower containing her soul
- catch bullets with their hands, lovely voices to sing
- send people to nonexistent places for the night
- sometimes benevolent, sometimes would apply curses - very hag quality
- sometimes ghostly - no feet

- unpredictable, bizarre fascinations - different morality system
- hoard magical secrets and knowledge
	- willing to share these secrets for a heavy, hidden price
	- feed on the suffering (or whatever emotion the hag is born from) through their deals

Ideas
- Appears to the party as a benevolent helper throughout the campaign
	- Gives cure to beloved character/NPC's incurable disease, in exchange for a flower that's in the middle of a large circular ruin somewhere where your next boss fight will be :)
		- Hag uses the flower for something evil nefarious; creating a spell that cripples/blights a town where someone was rude to her once
	- Is trying to use the party's gathered into to spy on her hags, and take the power that each is amassing
- She is tampering with eldritch powers, opening dimensional tears across the planes and a hostile alien virus is spilling through
	- The virus is similar to the x parasite in Metroid. Roiling masses of black biomass and tentacles that consume anything they touch, killing them incredibly quickly and hijacking their likeness and abilities although augmented to be stronger
		- chaos beasts
		- black beasts of bedlam
	- She wants to eventually destroy the cores of each of the elemental planes - where people draw their magic from - so that she can become even more powerful as mortals weaken (hags get their powers from emotions)
		- if a core is destroyed the plane to which it belongs begins to crumble, and spells from the corresponding element are inaccessible to mortal casters
- She wants to use the floating university to crash into the ground above an ancient temple/tomb where one of the primordial's has been locked away
	- Corrupt the engines that keep the university afloat
	- The impact will destroy the university and any nearby cities - the tomb is near the Sindalum capital so it would be destroyed in the impact blast
