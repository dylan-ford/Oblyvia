- The kingdom of Jihra was once proudly ruled by a strong and noble monarchy. The kings and queens of this lineage saw their station appointed to them by their god (sky/wind god), and vowed to honour their god's wishes by fighting as hard as they could for prosperity for their people. Nearby Jihra were the _whispered peaks_, tall, spindly mountains that housed temples of the Ai'shiman Order. This order comprised of nomadic monks, who worshipped a shared deity and sought peace and enlightenment. The Jihran monarchy revered these monks, and made a permanent place on their council for their leaders to advise the monarch on spiritual matters. All that changed with the birth of king Haemus IV, who as a child became obsessed with the stories from the Ai'shiman Order about masters reaching higher states of existance and transcendence through enlightenment. Through this obsession, nefarious members of the Ai'shiman Order saw an opportunity to seize power. They would spin lies to the king, amplifying stories of enlightenment and filling his head with delusion. He stayed in his room all day every day, practicing his meditations and shirking his kingly duties in order to learn the secrets of the universe and deliver them to his subjects. The advisors ruled in his stead, ensuring that no one else could ever go into the king's room, and began pushing their own twisted views and plans as 'the king's wishes.'
	- Formed a religion around themselves, declaring the advisors as disciples of the wind god themselves, and demanded fealty and worship by the subjects of Jihra
