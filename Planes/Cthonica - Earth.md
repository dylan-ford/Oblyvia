Plane made up almost entirely of solid earth and minerals
- The civilizations that dwell here make residence around the Mote Cores: (relatively) small planetary-like cores which provide warmth and light
- Cities are constructed by carving out enourmous spherical structures around each Mote
	- Within these hallowed spheres are layers of earth interspersed with floor-to-ceiling stalactites supporting homes and industry for Cthonica's denizens
Travel
- Besides established portals a common method of traveling to Cthonica is to swallow a precious gem and bury oneself completely in dirt and stone. The crushing force will shoot someone to the earthen plane and the gemstone will provide safe passage
