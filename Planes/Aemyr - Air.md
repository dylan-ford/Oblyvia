The plane of air, Aemyr, is an endless sea of gentle breezes, forceful gales, and buoyant clouds. It is fabled that Aemyr was once a primordial elemental titan with a body of winds so fierce they  shredded through the fabric of the material plane and created a new space for itself entirely.
- Dotted between the unpredictable gales of the plane are some earthen islands and cloudy cities where elementals and some races congregate to call home.
- Located at the very center of the eye of the storm that is Aemyr is the city of Steel which contains within it the Storm Heart, the eternal beating engine for the winds of the plane.
- The constantly shifting nature of the gales in this plane make life chaotic and unpredictable. Denizens here have found it useful to adopt mindsets which are spontaneous and resilient, as plans and destinations can change at Aemyr's whim

- with only a single sun, nights in Aemyr are almost pitch black, lest for the bioluminescent fruits that grow on trees, and attract fireflies which can be seen dancing around the night air
- even the clouds that cities are build on can turn stormy, providing a unique source of energy and power to the cities themselves, and airships often land on storm clouds to help recharge
- looking up in Aemyr sees a seemingly infinite dome of cloud above, with a single circular pocket allowing in light. Depending on the day this hole can expand, showing wide blue skies beyond or close up, leaving a dark and foreboding tapestry heralding storms. Looking
- on bright days the temperature is comfortable but is usually colder than is comfortable for most races
- sunsets paint the cloudy skies a technicolour blend of pinks, oranges, and purples and are said to be some of the most beautiful views in the multiverse
